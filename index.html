    Word bank (with Spanish)
    Cohesioncohesión

    Water molecules sticking to other water molecules.

    Adhesionadhesión

    Water sticking to a different material, like the penny.

    Hydrogen bondpuente de hidrógeno

    A weak pull between the H (+) of one water molecule and the O (−) of another. Dashed line.

    Polar covalent bondenlace covalente polar

    A strong bond inside one water molecule. Electrons are shared unequally between O and H. Solid line.

    Ionion

    An atom with a charge. Salt (NaCl) splits into Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions in water.

    Surface tensiontensión superficial

    Cohesion at the surface makes water act like a stretchy skin. It holds the dome up.
